# Cloth folding

**Keep the fold from the first touch of the cloth to its final release.** Cut everything else.

## Start

The first frame in which a finger touches the cloth. Judge contact in the gripper cameras (left and right), where the fingers are large and close.

## Stop

The first frame in which no finger touches the cloth, after its final release. The stop frame itself is not kept.

## Part of the task

- Moving the folded cloth aside, or setting it down, before the final release.

## Not part of the task

- Picking up the grippers before the first touch.
- Setting the grippers down after the final release.

## When it's unclear

- **Unclear boundary:** mark your best guess, press **Q**, choose "Unclear boundary" and say where in a short note.
- **Recording ends mid-task:** keep to the end, press **Q** and choose "Unfinished task".
- **No useful task at all:** press **N**.
- **Collection problem** (frozen camera, cameras out of sync, wrong procedure, hardware fault): press **Q** and choose "Collection problem". It goes back to the collection team.

## How this task is checked

A reviewer re-annotates a small share of the recordings without seeing yours. A start within about 10 frames (⅓ s) of the reviewer's and a stop within about 15 frames (½ s) counts as agreement; nobody expects the exact same frame. A missing or extra interval matters more than a few frames.
