# Example guideline: folding and cable plugging

Version 1 · recordings of 2026-10-09 (cloth folding) and 2026-10-06 (cable plugging)

This is an example of the one-page guideline a team lead writes for each batch. The annotation tool shows it next to the video and stamps its version on every save, but never interprets it: these rules are for people. The time links below open the tool at that moment.

## The rule

**Keep each task from the first touch of its item to the final release of that item.** Cut everything else.

- Picking up and putting down the grippers is not part of a task.
- A pause of more than about 3 s with nothing touched is cut: D at the release, S at the next touch. Shorter pauses stay inside the interval.
- Cables lying on the table as distractors never change keep or cut by themselves.

## Finding a boundary

1. Watch at 2× and press **S** at the first touch and **D** at the release. Don't stop to be exact.
2. When you reach the end, press **Enter** to visit each rough mark and put it on the first frame of the new state: **,** and **.** move it a frame (Shift: ten), a click on a filmstrip frame puts it there, or step with **←** and **→** and press **T**. Then **Enter** accepts it and goes to the next.
3. Judge contact in the gripper cameras (left and right), where the fingers are large and close. The start is the first frame in which a finger touches the item; the stop is the first frame in which none does. The stop frame itself is not kept.

## Cloth folding (2026-10-09)

- **Start:** the first touch of the cloth.
- **Stop:** the final release of the cloth. Moving the folded cloth aside (…09-40-56, …09-48-47) or setting it down in the middle (…10-04-08) happens before that release, so it is kept. Setting the grippers down afterwards is cut.
- Where to look in 2026-10-09--09-40-56: the fold starts about [0:02](#2026-10-09--09-40-56/59); the cloth is moved aside about [1:18](#2026-10-09--09-40-56/2338) and released about [1:20](#2026-10-09--09-40-56/2399); the grippers go down about [1:22](#2026-10-09--09-40-56/2458).

## Cable plugging (2026-10-06)

- **Each plug-in and each plug-out is its own interval.** Start at the first touch of the cable or the adapter; stop at the release once the plug is seated (plug-in) or free (plug-out).
- The head turns between attempts are cut, and so are rests with both grippers on the table. Hold **F** to skim them at 4×.
- Where to look: the first cycle starts after the operator turns to the table at about [0:26](#2026-10-06--10-11-23/780). Expect an interval every few seconds for the whole recording.

## When it's unclear

- **Unclear boundary:** mark your best guess, then press **Q**, choose "unclear boundary" and say where in a short note.
- **Recording ends mid-task:** keep to the end and press **Q** → "unfinished task".
- **No useful task at all:** press **N**.
- **Collection problem** (frozen camera, cameras out of sync, wrong procedure, hardware fault): **Q** → "collection problem". It goes back to the collection team.

## How your work is checked

A reviewer re-annotates a small share of recordings without seeing yours. A start within about 10 frames (⅓ s) of the reviewer's and a stop within about 15 frames (½ s) counts as agreement; nobody expects the exact same frame. A missing or extra interval matters more than a few frames of offset.
