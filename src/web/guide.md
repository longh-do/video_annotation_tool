# How to annotate

Keep the parts of the recording where the task happens, as the Task tab describes it, and cut the rest. Kept parts show in yellow on the timeline.

## 1. Mark while you watch

Play the video (`Space`). Press `S` or **Start** where the task starts and `D` or **Stop** where it stops.

## 2. Check the marks

Marks made while the video plays land a little late, so they show a **?** and *needs check*. Press `Enter`: the video stops on the next one, and a row of still frames from just before and after it appears below the video. Click the frame where the task really starts or stops (or move the mark with `,` and `.`), then `Enter` accepts it and goes to the next. A click on a camera on the right shows it large, which helps to see the fingers. Marks made while paused are exact already.

## 3. Submit

`⌘↵` or **Submit** saves and opens the next recording. Your work also saves by itself as you go.

## On the timeline

- Click to jump there; click an interval to select it.
- Drag the edge of an interval to move it.
- Click the round badge on a boundary to delete it.
- Right-click an interval to merge it with its neighbour or delete it.
- Pinch or `⌘+` / `⌘−` to zoom, scroll sideways to move, `⌘0` for the whole recording.

## When something is off

- `⌘Z` undoes anything, `⇧⌘Z` redoes it.
- Nothing worth keeping: `N`.
- Unsure, or a problem with the recording: `Q`, with a short note.
- Every command and its keys: `⌘K`.

On Windows and Linux, ⌘ is Ctrl.
